## English

**HyperMagic is free and open source (GPLv3). If you paid for it, you were robbed.**

Everything that changed since 0.2.0.

### New name: HyperMagic
- HyperXR is now **HyperMagic**: new name, new app package, credits for the partnership with Samrat Beketov (MagicXR).
- **New logo**, made with Samrat: app icon and both start-up animations. In VR, the logo is drawn in its own colours.
- MagicXR's pinch lock is now in HyperMagic.

### Languages
- **English, Spanish and Russian**, plus Portuguese. Every text is translated, and you choose the language in the first-run setup and in the settings.

### New interface (Quest style)
- **Quick Settings** like the Quest's: battery, date, volume and brightness, big cards and pill shortcuts.
- **New dock:** clock, battery and Wi-Fi block, your avatar, coloured squares and a tooltip with the name when you point at an icon.
- **Library** in the Horizon style: search, visible filters, covers and solid cards.
- **Teal theme** on every panel. The window bar is at the bottom (close, minimise, title), with a grab handle.
- In mixed reality the interface sits 35% further away, at the same viewing angle.
- **First-run setup** (2D): permissions, RealityLenses, eyes and hands calibration, Shizuku and gestures. You can run it again from Home.
- **Interactive tutorial** in XR: hands, pinch, palm menu and closed hand. Each step moves on when it sees the gesture.

### Store, People and Missions
- **Store:** every game and app is there, with a sidebar (All, genres, Minecraft, OpenXR, apps, installed), a highlight, "Start here", "New" and pages. HyperMagic games are downloaded from the Store (Download / Uninstall).
- **People** (Supabase): profile, friends, messages, calls and screen sharing. Calls use GeckoView's WebRTC.
- **Missions:** XP, levels, daily missions, streak and achievements.
- New **Camera** panel.

### Hands and arms
- **Human hand with skin:** a real 3D hand mesh with nails, creases and sleeve, GPU skinning (19 bones) and a skin shader. You can pick the skin tone.
- **Arm measured by the NPU** in VR: a skin and clothes segmenter (MediaPipe, NNAPI) gives the forearm's direction from the camera. The elbow is solved in the plane the camera sees, and shoulder IK is the fallback when the arm is not visible.
- **Right and left are never swapped:** the side is decided by the hand's 3D shape and position, not only by the detector's label, with hysteresis. An arm no longer jumps to the wrong shoulder.
- The hand and person cut-out was removed. It cut out your own body and cost NPU and GPU time.

### Controllers
- **Controller in your hand:** with your hand in view, a connected controller shows as a VR controller in the hand, which closes around the grip. The trigger and A click instead of the pinch.
- **New: colour-tracked 6DoF controller.** Stick coloured stickers on any controller.
  - In the calibrator, mark the spots, their colours, where the pointer goes and an optional button spot (cover it with a finger to click).
  - A GPU shader finds the colours and a solver computes the position and rotation: full 6DoF with 3 or more spots.
- **Two colour controllers at once** (for example two Joy-Cons): calibrate one as left and one as right. A Joy-Con keeps its buttons and stick, and its position comes from the colours.

### Games
- **30 new games** in the Store, inspired by Quest games: rhythm, sports, action, puzzle and adventure.
- **Macacada** (gorilla tag, online with other players), **Babuíno da Meia-Noite** (gorilla horror) and **Expediente** (a job simulator).
- **Tempo Parado** now plays with your hands.
- **Gorilla movement:** Another Axiom's official **GorillaLocomotion** (the movement of Gorilla Tag, MIT) is ported to Kotlin. The hands are steadied for a phone camera (One Euro filter, false jumps ignored, a lost hand holds for a moment), and a low hand reaches further down, so you can touch the ground.
- **Escalada** (climbing) is steadier: a flicker of the closed-hand gesture no longer drops you, and letting go of one hand no longer jerks the body.
- **New game: Escalada de Cano (Pipe Climb).** All you have is a pipe and you can't walk: hook it on bars and pull, plant it and push, vault over gaps. Balls hold the pipe in place of your hands.

### Minecraft Java
- **Our own Minecraft Java launcher**, ported from QuestCraft's Pojlib, with **QuestCraft** and **GorillaCraft** profiles. GorillaCraft is ViveMonkeCraft tuned for a VR Box.
- Fixed: QuestCraft's JVM aborted on `free` (native heap pointer tagging is now off).

### Stability
- **Native crash guard:** a native step that brought the app down (NPU or GPU of TFLite, drawing a game) is skipped the next time.
- **Games no longer take the app down:** an error closes only the game, with a report. A native crash in the first frames opens the game in safe mode next time.
- Fixed a light colour (RGBA) that closed Escalada, and `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` in HyperConnect.

### Notes
- Not everything was tested on every phone. The colour controller, the NPU arm, the official gorilla movement and Escalada de Cano were built and tested by automated tests but not yet on many devices. Please report what you find.
- Some features depend on the phone: ARCore, ARCore Depth, NPU, camera and sensors.
- Third-party licences: Kenney (CC0) models, Poly Haven (CC0) environments, Material Symbols (Apache-2.0), GorillaLocomotion (MIT, `third_party/GorillaLocomotion`), Pojlib (LGPL-3.0).
- HyperMagic is free software under the **GNU GPL v3.0**. Selling it without the source code violates the licence.

- **Предплечье, измеренное NPU** в VR: сегментация кожи и одежды (MediaPipe, NNAPI) даёт направление предплечья по камере. Локоть находится в плоскости, видимой камерой; если рука не видна, работает IK от плеча.

### Контроллеры

- **Новое: 6DoF-контроллер по цвету.** Наклейте цветные наклейки на любой контроллер.
  - В калибровке отметьте точки, их цвета, куда идёт указатель и, по желанию, точку-кнопку (закройте её пальцем, чтобы нажать).
  - Шейдер на GPU находит цвета, а решатель вычисляет положение и поворот: полное 6DoF с 3 и более точками.
- **Два цветных контроллера одновременно** (например, два Joy-Con): откалибруйте один как левый, другой как правый. Joy-Con сохраняет свои кнопки и стик, а положение берётся по цветам.

- **Escalada** más estable: un parpadeo del gesto de mano cerrada ya no te hace caer, y soltar una mano ya no da un tirón al cuerpo.
- **Juego nuevo: Escalada de Cano (Escalada con Tubo).** Solo tienes un tubo y no puedes caminar: engánchalo en las barras y tira, apóyalo y empuja, salta los huecos. Unas bolas sujetan el tubo en lugar de tus manos.

### Minecraft Java
- **Launcher propio de Minecraft Java**, portado del Pojlib de QuestCraft, con los perfiles **QuestCraft** y **GorillaCraft**. GorillaCraft es ViveMonkeCraft ajustado para VR Box.
- Corregido: la JVM de QuestCraft abortaba en `free` (se desactivó el etiquetado de punteros del heap nativo).

### Estabilidad
- **Protección contra crashes nativos:** un paso nativo que tumbó la app (NPU o GPU de TFLite, dibujo de un juego) se omite la próxima vez.
- **Los juegos ya no tumban la app:** un error cierra solo el juego, con un informe. Un crash nativo en los primeros fotogramas hace que el juego se abra en modo seguro la próxima vez.
- Corregidos el color de luz (RGBA) que cerraba Escalada y el `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` en HyperConnect.

### Avisos
- No todo se probó en todos los móviles. El mando por color, el brazo por NPU, el movimiento oficial de gorila y Escalada de Cano pasaron la compilación y las pruebas automáticas, pero aún no en muchos dispositivos. Cuéntanos lo que encuentres.
